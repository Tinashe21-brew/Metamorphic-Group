About Metamorphic Group

Built to turn logistics pressure into on-time outcomes

Metamorphic Group launched two years ago on a simple premise: freight across Zimbabwe that arrives when we say it will. In that time we've held a 99.99% on-time delivery rate and kept 100% of our clients.

A fleet of 6 cars and 12 motorbikes moves cargo through Harare and out to Mutare, Bulawayo, Kariba and the routes between them, backed by warehousing in major cities and dispatch support that runs 24/7.
